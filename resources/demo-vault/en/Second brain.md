A second brain is an archive of your own notes, linked together, that extends your memory. Each note expresses a single concept and points to others: like a [[Neuron]] with its [[Synapse]]s.

The [[Zettelkasten]] method is the best-known example. Here you can also talk to your notes thanks to [[Language models]]s.
