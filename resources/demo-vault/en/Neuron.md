The neuron is the basic cell of the nervous system: it receives signals through its dendrites, adds them up and, if they pass a threshold, fires an impulse along its axon.

Neurons talk to each other through [[Synapse]]s. A human brain holds about 86 billion of them.

They are the direct inspiration for the [[Artificial neural network]]: each node of the network imitates, in a very simplified way, a neuron.
