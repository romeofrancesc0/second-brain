# Welcome to your Second Brain

This is a demo vault: a few linked notes to show how the app works. Every note is a **neuron**, every `[[link]]` is a **synapse**, every course has its own colour.

## Things to try

- Rotate with the mouse, zoom with the wheel, fly with `W` `A` `S` `D`.
- Click a neuron: the camera flies to it and you can read the note on the right.
- Press `⌘K` (or `Ctrl+K`) to search. Press `1` `2` `3` to switch view.
- Press `⌘J` (or `Ctrl+J`) to talk to the agent: it follows the rules written in [[AGENTS]]. Try: *"How are the brain and neural networks connected?"* or *"Which links are missing from the Embedding note?"*
