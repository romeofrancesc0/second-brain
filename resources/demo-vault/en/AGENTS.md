# Vault rules

Second Brain demo vault: short concepts on the brain, artificial intelligence and study methods.

- One note = one concept, in English, a few lines long.
- Frontmatter: `corso` (one of Neuroscience, Artificial Intelligence, Learning Better) and `tags`.
- Every new note links at least two existing notes with `[[...]]` and is added to the MOC of its course.
