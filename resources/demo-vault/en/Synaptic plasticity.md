Synapses that are used often get stronger, those that are ignored get weaker. “Neurons that fire together, wire together” (Hebb).

It is how the [[Hippocampus]] fixes new memories and what [[Long-term memory]] relies on to last.

[[Backpropagation]] is the artificial equivalent: it adjusts the strength of the connections to reduce the error.
