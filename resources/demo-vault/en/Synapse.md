The synapse is the point of contact between two [[Neuron]]s: the signal passes from one to the other through neurotransmitters.

The strength of a synapse is not fixed: it changes with use. This mechanism is called [[Synaptic plasticity]] and is the physical basis of learning.

In this app every link between two notes is a synapse.
